# Africa Wave 1 — currentness alerts and document preflights

**Research checked:** 9 November 2026  
**State:** Research only. No text in this file is approved for import or publication.

This note supplements [Africa Wave 1 source register](../africa-wave1-constitution-source-register.md). It records amendment developments that make older PDFs unsafe to call current.

## 1. South Africa — Eighteenth Amendment commencement in 2026

**Primary source route**
- [Department of Justice — constitutional amendment history](https://www.justice.gov.za/constitution/amendments.html)
- [Parliament — Constitution and amendments](https://parliament.gov.za/constitutional-amendments)
- [Government Gazette search result — Proclamation 321 / 2026](https://www.gov.za/document?page=0&search_query=constitution)

**Currentness finding**
- The Constitution Eighteenth Amendment Act 3 of 2023 was assented to on 26 March 2026.
- Proclamation 321 of 2026, Government Gazette 54901, commenced the Act on 26 June 2026.
- Proclamation 324 of 2026, Government Gazette 54933, issued an erratum on 2 July 2026.
- The Department of Justice amendment history lists the Eighteenth Amendment Act and identifies the Nineteenth through Twenty-Fourth Amendment proposals as Bills, not enacted amendments.
- The familiar Constitutional Court Library consolidated text explicitly says it includes amendments only through the Seventeenth Amendment and is not an official government publication. It is therefore not current for CARTA.

**CARTA action**
- Obtain the official text of Act 3 of 2023, Proclamation 321 and erratum 324 from the Gazette.
- Apply only the commenced amendments to the authoritative 1996 text.
- Independently compare the resulting consolidation against the Government/Parliament/Justice sources.
- Keep the document in `version_review_required` until that reconciliation is signed off.
- Rights review remains separate; a public government page is not blanket commercial/AI/offline permission.

## 2. Zambia — Constitution Amendment Act No. 13 of 2025

**Primary source route**
- [Official constitutional reform portal — Act No. 13 of 2025](https://constitution.gov.zm/act-13-2025)
- [National Assembly — Constitution of Zambia](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/4831)

**Currentness finding**
- The official reform portal identifies Act No. 13 of 2025 as the Constitution of Zambia (Amendment) Act, 2025 and records presidential assent on 18 December 2025.
- The older 2016 constitutional text is not a complete current edition.
- ZambiaLII's 18 December 2025 consolidated record is a useful amendment cross-check, but it is a secondary host and cannot alone establish CARTA reuse rights.

**CARTA action**
- Obtain the official Act PDF and confirm commencement / transitional provisions against the Gazette.
- Build the consolidated Constitution from the official 2016 constitutional text plus the 2025 Act and any other later amendments.
- Record the effective date per amended provision; do not simply set every provision to the Act's assent date.
- Keep rights status blocked pending a documented reuse basis.

## 3. Botswana — 2025 amendment requires commencement check

**Discovery lead**
- [Constitution (Amendment) Act, 2025 — Gazette copy indexed by search](https://ewm.eusee.global/assets/gravity_forms/1-0d0cb925a448c9c23b984f4a631719ee/2025/08/Government-Gazette-Extraordinary-04.07.25.pdf)
- [Government of Botswana](https://www.gov.bw/)

**Currentness finding**
- The 2025 amendment text states that it comes into operation on a date appointed by Ministerial Order published in the Gazette.
- The located PDF does not establish the appointed commencement date. It must not be assumed to be in force merely because the Act was published or assented to.
- The official government constitutional text and the 2025 commencement order still need to be located and checked.

**CARTA action**
- Ask the Government Printer / Attorney-General's Chambers for the authoritative current text and commencement order.
- Maintain the 2025 Act as a separate amendment instrument until commencement is proven.
- Do not merge its provisions into a purported current Constitution without the commencement evidence.

## 4. Malawi — consolidated text through 31 January 2025 located

**Source leads**
- [Malawi Constitution consolidated to 31 January 2025](https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/resources/THE%20CONSTITUTION%20OF%20THE%20REPUBLIC%20OF%20MALAWI.pdf)
- [Ministry of Justice older Constitution PDF](https://justice.gov.mw/sites/default/files/2021-06/Malawi%20Constitution.pdf)

**Currentness finding**
- The Fourth Revised Edition describes itself as consolidating amendments through 31 January 2025.
- Its preface says the consolidation is an authoritative reference but does not replace the official Gazette text, which remains the Constitution as published in 1994 and amended by separate Acts and Law Revision Orders.
- The older Ministry of Justice PDF only states amendment coverage up to Act No. 38 of 1998 and is not a current consolidated text.

**CARTA action**
- Obtain the 2025 Fourth Revised Edition's publication metadata and confirm its publisher / legal status with the Malawi Law Commission or Ministry of Justice.
- Reconcile it against the Gazette and any post-31 January 2025 amendments.
- Check rights for the official Gazette text and the editorial consolidation separately; do not assume the consolidation's editorial material is free to reproduce.

## 5. Ghana — 2026 review proposals are not yet proof of enacted amendments

**Source leads**
- [Judicial Service — 1992 Constitution](https://www.judicial.gov.gh/index.php/the-constitution)
- [Office of the Special Prosecutor — Constitution PDF](https://osp.gov.gh/media/Resources/Ghana_Constitution.pdf)
- [Presidency — 2025 Constitution Review Committee report and proposals, published 30 July 2026](https://presidency.gov.gh/report-of-the-constitution-review-committee-2025-proposals-for-amendment-of-the-1992-constitution/)

**Currentness finding**
- Official hosts provide the 1992 Constitution, but the PDF found on the Office of the Special Prosecutor site is an older file.
- The Presidency published a Constitution Review Committee report and proposals for amendment on 30 July 2026. A review report or proposal is not itself an enacted constitutional amendment.
- The amendment Acts, Gazette and current consolidated text must be reconciled before marking current.

**CARTA action**
- Obtain a certified or official current consolidation and amendment list from Parliament / Attorney-General / Government Printer.
- Check whether any recommendations have since become Bills or enacted amendment Acts.
- Keep all review proposals separate from the in-force constitutional text.

## 6. Zimbabwe — 2026 amendment alert

**Discovery leads**
- [Veritas Acts index](https://www.veritaszim.net/acts?page=4)
- [Veritas consolidated 2023 Constitution](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Constitution%20Consolidated%20%282023%29.pdf)

**Currentness finding**
- Veritas's Acts index lists a Constitution of Zimbabwe Amendment Act No. 6 of 2026 and a consolidated 2026 text.
- Veritas is a respected legal-information research source, but a Veritas consolidation is not the official Gazette.
- The 2023 consolidated PDF is no longer a safe candidate for currentness without checking the 2026 amendment and all subsequent official publications.

**CARTA action**
- Locate Act No. 6 of 2026 in the Zimbabwe Government Gazette / Parliament / Attorney-General's official publication.
- Confirm assent, commencement and exact provisions changed.
- Obtain the official consolidated text or have a qualified Zimbabwean legal editor reconcile the Gazette Acts.
- Rights clearance remains separate.

## 7. Namibia — current consolidated secondary copy found; official consolidation still needed

**Source leads**
- [Government of Namibia Constitution PDF](https://www.gov.na/documents/146489/641903/Namibia_Constitution.pdf/d2d4c4cd-3f19-ab0b-8bdb-8d5a5da3ee79)
- [NamibLII Constitution record](https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/1990/constitution/eng%402014-10-13)

**Currentness finding**
- The Government-hosted PDF is an official source lead, but the retrieved document does not establish a 2026 consolidated amendment cut-off.
- NamibLII describes a version from 13 October 2014 and says it includes amendments published up to 26 June 2026. That is a useful secondary cross-check, not the official publisher's certification or a reuse licence.

**CARTA action**
- Request an official consolidated text and amendment schedule from the Government Printer / Ministry of Justice.
- Compare every amendment in the secondary consolidated record with official Gazette Acts.
- Keep the NamibLII copy out of the published corpus unless its terms independently permit the intended use.

## General decision

These findings show why a single search-result PDF cannot be counted as a verified constitution. For every candidate, CARTA must retain:

- the exact official base text;
- every amendment instrument and commencement evidence;
- a consolidated-through date;
- authoritative-language rule;
- source and rights evidence;
- a reviewer sign-off and SHA-256 checksum.

No document has been imported or made public by this research. The live source/document rights gates remain unchanged.
