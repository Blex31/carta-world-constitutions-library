# Africa 54-state constitution coverage tracker

**Snapshot:** 9 November 2026  
**Scope:** 54 African UN Member States. This is a coverage tracker, not a claim that 54 verified current constitutional texts have been collected.

## Coverage rules

- One jurisdiction can have more than one constitutional instrument. Zanzibar is tracked separately from the Union Constitution of Tanzania; historical, transitional and interim instruments must not be mislabeled as permanent constitutions.
- A country is counted as covered only when at least one correct in-force constitutional instrument has passed currentness, rights, completeness, checksum and review gates and is available through CARTA's reader.
- A source URL, metadata record, PDF download or AI-generated summary does not count as coverage.
- Where no single codified constitution exists, the product must describe the constitutional framework accurately rather than inventing a single constitution.

- **Wave 1 preflight:** 15 candidate jurisdictions; see [source register](africa-wave1-constitution-source-register.md). Currentness alerts are in [currentness alerts](country-research/africa-wave1-currentness-alerts.md).
