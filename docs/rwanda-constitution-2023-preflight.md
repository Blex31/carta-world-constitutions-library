## 1. Official text identified

- **Official title:** Constitution of the Republic of Rwanda
- **Gazette:** Official Gazette n° Special of 04/08/2023
- **Official publisher/custodian:** Government of Rwanda; Official Gazette managed by the Quality Assurance and Official Gazette Directorate General, Ministry of Justice.
- **RLRC copy:** https://www.rlrc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RLRC/Laws_of_Rwanda_v2/Domestic_laws/Laws_in_force/1._Fundamental/1.1._National_Instruments/1.1.1._Constitution/1.1.1.1._Constitution_of_the_Rep._of_Rwanda_2023.pdf
- **Parliament copy:** https://www.parliament.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Parliament/STATUTORY_DOCUMENTS/The_Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_Rwanda.pdf
- **Languages shown in the Gazette:** Kinyarwanda, English and French. Article 8 identifies all three as official languages. Article 96(4) says that where published language versions of a law conflict, the language in which the law was adopted prevails. RLRC/counsel must confirm the adoption language for this Constitution; do not assume that every translation controls equally.
- **Structure:** the Gazette text contains 176 articles; Article 176 provides for entry into force upon presidential promulgation and publication in the Official Gazette.
- **RLRC current-law statement:** RLRC says its Laws of Rwanda collection contains legislation currently in force and is updated daily by adding new legislation and removing repealed legislation.

The RLRC-hosted document is listed in its current Laws in force collection. The Parliament copy is a second official-hosted copy with the same Gazette date and language structure. These establish a strong official-source candidate, but a publisher response is still needed to confirm the current consolidation and language hierarchy as at 5 November 2026.

## 2. Amendment and currentness review

The 2023 Gazette publication is the identified revision. Parliament's official news report of 2 June 2023 says the revision changed eight articles in substance, modified 12 stylistically and retained a 176-article Constitution. The 2026 Official Gazette n° Special of 03/03/2026 contains Organic Law n° 001/2026.OL determining the functioning of the Senate; the Constitution is referenced in that law, but the Gazette instrument itself is an Organic Law, not a constitutional amendment.

A targeted search of the Rwanda Official Gazette index and official sources for 2024–2026 did not locate a later constitutional amendment. This is not conclusive proof that none exists. Request written confirmation from RLRC / the Official Gazette Directorate and have a Rwanda-qualified lawyer verify the Gazette record before setting `is_current=true`.

## 3. Copyright/reuse finding — important update

Law N° 055/2024 of 20/06/2024 on the Protection of Intellectual Property:

- was published in Official Gazette n° Special of 31/07/2024;
- entered into force on its Gazette publication date (Article 387);
- repealed Law No. 31/2009 (Article 386);
- Article 282 says protection does not extend to official legislative, administrative and judicial texts and official translations of those texts.

WIPO Lex hosts the 2024 law and identifies its dates; the Gazette PDF contains Article 282 and the final provisions. This is a strong statutory basis to assess the official constitutional text and its official translations. It is **not yet a completed CARTA rights approval**. Obtain Rwanda counsel's written opinion on applying Article 282 to this particular Constitution and the intended uses, and distinguish the text from the publisher's PDF artwork, seals, logos, website/database features, editorial annotations and any non-official translations.

CARTA's intended uses requiring explicit scope review:
- full-text web and mobile display;
- full-text search and indexing;
- quotation/excerpts and provision-level citation;
- AI retrieval, embeddings and summarisation;
- offline storage/download in Android/iOS apps;
- commercial use through a paid subscription.

Do not use the repealed 2009 IP law's former Article 198 as the current legal basis.
