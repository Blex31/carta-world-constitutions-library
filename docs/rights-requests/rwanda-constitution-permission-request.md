# Rwanda Constitution — publisher and rights confirmation request

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Primary contact:** Rwanda Law Reform Commission (RLRC), `info@rlrc.gov.rw`  
**Request:** Please forward this to the Quality Assurance and Official Gazette Directorate General / the competent Ministry of Justice office if RLRC is not the appropriate decision-maker.

## Copy-ready request

**Subject: Request for confirmation of current official Constitution text and permitted digital reuse — CARTA**

Dear Rwanda Law Reform Commission team,

We are preparing CARTA — World Constitutions Library, a legal-information product intended to make official constitutional and legislative texts easier to browse, search and understand. CARTA is planned for web browsers and native Android and iOS applications, using a shared document catalogue and searchable legal-text service.

We have located the *Constitution of the Republic of Rwanda*, revised in 2023, in Official Gazette n° Special of 04/08/2023. We found the trilingual official publication (Kinyarwanda, English and French) through the Rwanda Law Reform Commission and a matching Parliament-hosted copy:

- RLRC official-text copy: https://www.rlrc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RLRC/Laws_of_Rwanda_v2/Domestic_laws/Laws_in_force/1._Fundamental/1.1._National_Instruments/1.1.1._Constitution/1.1.1.1._Constitution_of_the_Rep._of_Rwanda_2023.pdf
- Parliament-hosted copy: https://www.parliament.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Parliament/STATUTORY_DOCUMENTS/The_Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_Rwanda.pdf

We also note that Law N° 055/2024 of 20/06/2024 on the Protection of Intellectual Property was published in Official Gazette n° Special of 31/07/2024. Article 282 states that copyright protection does not extend to official texts of a legislative, administrative or judicial nature, or official translations of such texts.

Before we publish or process the document, could you please confirm the following:

1. **Current text:** Is the 04/08/2023 Gazette text still the current consolidated Constitution as at 5 November 2026? Have any constitutional amendments, corrigenda, commencement notices or official corrections been published since then?
2. **Authoritative versions:** Article 8 identifies Kinyarwanda, English and French as official languages. Article 96(4) says that when language versions of a law published in the Official Gazette conflict, the language in which the law was adopted prevails. Which language was the 2023 constitutional revision adopted in, and which text must CARTA treat as controlling if the three versions differ?
3. **Article 282:** Does the exclusion in Article 282 of Law N° 055/2024 apply to the official text of the Constitution in the 04/08/2023 Gazette and to its official translations?
4. **Digital use:** Are there any separate restrictions, permissions, fees or conditions applicable to extracting and reproducing the official statutory text (not the PDF's graphic design, logos, seals or website layout) in a searchable web and mobile legal library?
5. **Commercial and technical uses:** Does the statutory-text exclusion permit commercial display, full-text search/indexing, short provision excerpts, machine-assisted retrieval and summarisation, and user-authorised offline downloads/caching in CARTA's Android and iOS applications? Please identify any use that requires separate authorisation.
6. **Attribution and updates:** What publisher attribution, canonical link, version notice and update/correction process should CARTA use? Is there an official stable download, API, or notification channel for new or amended instruments?

We will preserve the Gazette citation, publisher attribution, official source link, document version, effective-date information and a checksum of the imported text. We will not reproduce official seals or the Gazette's page design. We will keep the document unpublished until the current version and applicable reuse position have been confirmed.

A written response or referral to the competent office would be greatly appreciated. Please also advise whether you require a formal institutional request or any additional documentation.

Kind regards,

**CARTA — World Constitutions Library project team**

## Internal review notes — do not send

- RLRC contact page lists `info@rlrc.gov.rw` and identifies RLRC's role in codifying and coordinating Rwandan laws.
- The Ministry of Justice says its Official Gazette directorate prepares and publishes the Gazette.
- Article 282 of the 2024 IP law is a promising statutory basis for the official text itself. Do not classify the source as `public_domain_verified` or `commercial_use_allowed=true` until qualified local counsel confirms the exact scope for CARTA's planned web/mobile, AI and offline uses and the publisher/version checks are complete.
- The 2009 IP law was repealed by Article 386 of Law N° 055/2024. Use the 2024 law, not the older Article 198, for the current review.
