# Draft rights and licensing request — Constitution of Kenya, 2010

**To:** National Council for Law Reporting (NCLR) / Kenya Law  
**Suggested contact route:** info@kenyalaw.org (confirm the current licensing contact before sending)  
**Subject:** Request for commercial digital and AI-use licence — Constitution of Kenya, 2010

Dear National Council for Law Reporting,

We are developing CARTA — World Constitutions Library, a legal-information platform that helps readers find and understand constitutional texts through a web application and native Android and iOS applications.

We are requesting written licensing terms and, subject to your approval, a licence covering the **exact official edition of the Constitution of Kenya, 2010** that NCLR confirms is current and suitable for republication.

CARTA is operated as a commercial digital service. We therefore understand that the non-commercial permissions described in the Kenya Law Terms of Use do not, by themselves, cover our intended use. We will not scrape, bulk-extract, redistribute, index for AI or publish the text commercially without an explicit written agreement or a separately documented legal basis.

## 1. Source and version requested

Please identify:
- The canonical NCLR record and download URL for the current consolidated Constitution of Kenya, 2010.
- The edition/revision label and date, including the amendment cut-off.
- The authoritative language version(s), and any language precedence rule.
- The enacted constitutional amendment instruments and commencement notices included in that consolidation.
- The best official Gazette or publisher reference to use for independent verification.

## 2. Rights requested

Please advise whether NCLR can grant CARTA permission to:

1. Download, copy, store, version and checksum the exact official text.
2. Display and reproduce the Constitution in CARTA's website and native Android/iOS apps.
3. Make the complete text available to both free and paid CARTA users as part of a commercial subscription product.
4. Reproduce individual Articles, schedules, excerpts and search snippets, with NCLR attribution.
5. Parse the text into structured chapters, parts, Articles and clauses; create a full-text index; and provide search/navigation.
6. Create embeddings and use the licensed text in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflows so that an AI assistant can answer questions using exact provision citations.
7. Generate summaries and plain-language explanations grounded in the text, with clear labelling that these are CARTA explanations and not official legal text or legal advice.
8. Translate or commission translations of the text, subject to any separate approvals NCLR requires. All translations and derivatives would be labelled unofficial unless authorised otherwise.
9. Provide encrypted offline copies on users' devices, with versioning, expiry and update controls.
10. Retain technical audit copies, checksums, source metadata and previous versions needed to preserve citation integrity and verify amendments.

We are not requesting permission to train a general-purpose machine-learning model on the text unless NCLR expressly offers and approves that additional use. Please state whether embeddings, retrieval, inference-time context, summarisation and related processing are treated separately from model training under your licensing policy.

## 3. Requested licence terms

Please provide the applicable:
- Licence fee, annual fee, per-document fee or revenue-share terms.
- Attribution text, placement and required links.
- Permitted user geography, number of users, applications and domains.
- Rights to store copies with cloud infrastructure and technical service providers.
- Rules for excerpts, caching, downloads and offline use.
- Restrictions on commercial subscriptions, advertisements or bundled services.
- Allowed uses for AI retrieval, embeddings, summarisation and translation.
- Update obligations, correction notifications and amendment feeds.
- Expiry, termination, takedown, archival and post-termination deletion requirements.
- Any restrictions on derived plain-language materials or provision-level structured datasets.

Please also advise whether NCLR offers an API, bulk data feed, update notification service or a licensing agreement designed for legal-information platforms.

## 4. CARTA safeguards

CARTA intends to:
- Display publisher provenance, version date, effective date and attribution alongside every text.
- Preserve the official text separately from unofficial summaries and translations.
- Show amendment history and warn users when a newer verified version is available.
- Cite the exact Article and clause used by legal research features.
- Disable access to a text if its licence expires or NCLR requires it to be withdrawn.
- Keep offline availability disabled unless the licence explicitly permits it.
- Avoid representing CARTA summaries as official government interpretations or as legal advice.

We would be grateful for the applicable licensing documents, fee schedule and the correct officer or department to contact for this request.
