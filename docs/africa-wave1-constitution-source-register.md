# Africa Wave 1 constitutional source register

**Research snapshot:** 9 November 2026  
**Purpose:** record official-source leads and version risks for CARTA's first African collection.  
**Publication status:** research only. No row below is approved for text ingestion, commercial display, AI retrieval, translation or offline distribution.

## Non-negotiable publication rule

An official URL is a discovery lead, not a licence. Before a document enters CARTA's public corpus, the exact edition must pass all gates in [constitution ingestion](constitution-ingestion.md) and [import playbook](import-playbook.md): authoritative publisher, current text and amendment cut-off, language precedence, complete text, rights basis for each intended use, legal/editorial review, checksum and importer dry-run.

**Never scrape or import all linked PDFs as a batch.** In particular, discovery repositories such as Constitute, ULII, ZambiaLII, NamibLII and Veritas are useful for cross-checking but their hosted copies and terms must not be assumed to authorise CARTA's commercial use.

## Status legend

- `source_lead_found`: a government, parliament, court or official legal-reform publisher page has been located.
- `version_review_required`: no confirmed current consolidated text or complete amendment cut-off yet.
- `rights_review_required`: commercial display, indexing, AI retrieval, derivative/plain-language work and offline copying are not cleared.
- `legal_hold`: material constitutional-status or version dispute requires specialist review.
- **None of these statuses means approved or imported.**

## Special constitutional-status holds

Do not force these into a single permanent-constitution slot without instrument-specific legal review:

- **Somalia:** 2012 Provisional Constitution / 2026 constitutional process and disputed amendments.
- **South Sudan:** transitional constitution and amendment/consolidation lineage.
- **Tanzania:** Union Constitution and Zanzibar Constitution are separate instruments.
- **Sudan:** transitional instruments and competing constitutional positions.
- **Eritrea:** distinguish the 1997 ratified text from whether it entered into force.
- **Libya and Niger:** distinguish permanent constitutions from interim constitutional declarations or transitional charters.
- **Guinea and Equatorial Guinea:** require specific rights review; do not infer commercial rights from a general regional copyright index.

## Rights and ingestion tracker

For every country, maintain separate fields for:
1. official publisher URL and document identifier;
2. exact instrument title, version date, publication and commencement dates;
3. consolidated-through date and amendments checked;
4. official languages and controlling language rule;
5. rights basis and evidence URL;
6. permission for commercial web/mobile display;
7. permission/legal basis for search, indexing and excerpts;
8. permission/legal basis for AI retrieval, embeddings and summaries;
9. permission/legal basis for translations/plain-language derivatives;
10. permission/legal basis for offline caching and user downloads;
11. attribution, licence conditions, expiry and takedown obligations;
12. reviewer, review date, checksum, import dry-run and publication approval.

Do not mark a source active or `commercial_use_allowed=true` merely because a government website hosts a PDF. Do not insert a document as `verified_current` until the exact text has been checked against official publications and amendment instruments.
